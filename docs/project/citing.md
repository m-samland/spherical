# Citing

If spherical supports your research, cite these papers.

- spherical: [Samland (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08044), in review at the
  Journal of Open Source Software.
- The IFS cube extraction:
  [Samland et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202244587).
- TRAP post-processing:
  [Samland et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937308).
- The species package, used for spectral templates:
  [Stolker et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937159).

The astrometric and photometric calibration of both pipelines, and parts of the IRDIS
reduction, build on A. Vigan's SPHERE tools
([repository](https://github.com/avigan/SPHERE), [Vigan 2020](https://ascl.net/2009.002)).
Please acknowledge them as well.

